# Business Entities

## Project
Represents a real-estate development or commercial project.

## Phase
Optional subdivision of a project.

## Building
Optional physical building/cluster/block.

## Unit
Sellable inventory item such as apartment, villa, office, retail unit, clinic, garage, or other property.

## Lead
Initial customer/prospect record.

## Opportunity
Qualified sales opportunity.

## Reservation
Temporary hold or booking against a unit.

## Contract
Executed commercial agreement between company and customer.

## Payment Schedule
Contractual installments and due dates.

## Receipt / Collection
Actual customer payment.

## Cheque
Cheque instrument related to a due installment or receipt.

## Broker
External or internal brokerage source.

## Commission
Commercial entitlement calculated from approved rules.

## Salesperson / Team
Internal commercial owner.

## Customer
Contracting party.
